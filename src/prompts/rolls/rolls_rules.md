# Rolls Rules (Stub)

## Purpose
Define when a player action should trigger a dice roll, and when it should not.

---

## Categories

### 1. No Roll
Cases where narration is enough.  
*(e.g. casual speech, looking around, low-stakes description)*

### 2. Auto-Success
Trivial actions that always succeed.  
*(e.g. picking up an object at your feet when calm)*

### 3. Auto-Fail
Actions impossible under world physics or current scenario limits.  
*(e.g. trying to fly without aid, leaving a locked demo area)*

### 4. Fixed DC
Actions that test ability against an environmental or set difficulty.  
*(e.g. climb, jump, force a door)*

### 5. Opposed
Actions contested by another creature or resisting force.  
*(e.g. attack vs dodge, shove vs strength)*

---

## Criticals
Nat 20 = strong success.  
Nat 1 = serious stumble.  
*(Narrator never shows numbers — only consequences.)*

---

## Debug Mode
If the player types **“debug please”**, append one short `[dbg: …]` line showing rolls and outcomes.
