# Combat Dynamics and Action Resolution

## Overview

This document outlines the structure and mechanics of combat and action resolution in the game. The process involves selecting targets, choosing actions, and resolving outcomes through attribute-based rolls and modifiers.

## Combat Structure

1. **Target Selection**
   - Players must choose one or more targets for their action.
   - Target selection can influence the success and outcome of the action.

2. **Action Selection**
   - Players choose an action type: Attack, Spell, or Skill.
   - Each action type has unique attributes and modifiers affecting its success.

3. **Roll for Success**
   - A roll is made to determine the success of the chosen action.
   - The roll is influenced by player attributes and action-specific modifiers.

## Action Resolution

### Attack Actions

1. **Success Roll**
   - Determine if the attack hits the target(s) based on attributes and modifiers.

2. **Damage Calculation**
   - If successful, calculate damage using the attack's base damage and any additional modifiers.

3. **Additional Effects**
   - Some attacks may have additional effects resolved through further rolls.

### Spell Actions

1. **Success Roll**
   - Determine if the spell successfully affects the target(s).

2. **Effect Resolution**
   - Resolve the spell's effects, which may include damage, healing, or status changes.

3. **Additional Rolls**
   - Some spells may require additional rolls to determine the extent or duration of effects.

### Skill Actions

1. **Success Roll**
   - Determine if the skill is executed successfully.

2. **Outcome Resolution**
   - Resolve the skill's outcome, which may vary widely depending on the skill type.

3. **Additional Steps**
   - Skills may involve additional steps or rolls to fully resolve their effects.

## Conclusion

The combat and action resolution system is designed to provide a dynamic and engaging experience, with each action type offering unique challenges and outcomes. Further details on specific actions, spells, and skills will be documented separately.
