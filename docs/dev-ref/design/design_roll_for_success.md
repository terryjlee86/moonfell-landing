
## Design for Roll Sucsess Mechanics 

1. Success or Failure Determination:
A D20 dice roll is used to determine success or failure.
Two main variables influence the outcome:
Required Number: The target number that the roll must meet or exceed for a success.
Actual Roll Result: The number rolled on the D20, potentially modified by various factors.

2. Influences on the Required Number:
The base success threshold (e.g., 5) can be adjusted by:
Target Attributes: Characteristics of the target that make it harder or easier to succeed (e.g., being in cover adds a difficulty modifier).
Modifiers: Additional factors that can increase or decrease the required number

3. Influences on the Roll Result:
The roll result can be modified by:
Attacker Attributes: Characteristics of the attacker that enhance or reduce their effectiveness.
Modifiers: Skills or conditions that provide bonuses or penalties (e.g., the "steady aim" skill adds a bonus to the roll).

4. Example:
Default success threshold: 5
Target in cover: +3 to the required number, making it 8.
Roll: 6
Attacker under "steady aim" skill: +5 to the roll, making it 11.
Since 11 is greater than 8, the attack is successful.
